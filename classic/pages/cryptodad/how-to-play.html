THE GOAL

You are a father trying to keep your family together while surviving in a brutal world of debt, crypto volatility, and bad decisions.

Every week you make choices. The longer you survive with high Family and low Heat, the better your ending will be.

Good play = long life + family stays intact + possible good ending.

Bad play = Heat spirals -> police, divorce, foreclosure, prison, or death.

Typical family state - protect this stat at all costs
YOUR STATS (THE FOUR PILLARS)

FAMILY - How happy and stable your home life is. Low Family = more incidents, possible divorce.

HEAT - Police attention and street reputation. High Heat = raids, arrests, family drama.

CASH / LIQUID - Money you can spend right now. Goes down from bills, actions, and bad events.

DEBT - What you owe. Interest accrues. If you hit zero cash with high debt, you may trigger Last Stand.

You also have a small crypto portfolio (BTC, ETH, SOL, MEME) that you can trade.

Left: Family high & stable * Right: High Heat (bad events incoming)
WEEKLY ACTIONS (THE MAIN MENU)

1. TRADE
Buy or sell crypto. Prices move every week (sometimes violently). MEME coins are extremely risky.
24-hour market window per week: each trade action burns in-game hours (stable/meme buys ~4h, sells ~3h, transfers ~1h). When the 24h clock hits zero, the week auto-advances - you cannot sit in trade forever stacking without time passing.

2. HUSTLE
Make money. This is the most important (and dangerous) choice.

3. FAMILY TIME
The single most important action in the game.
Spend time with your wife and kids. Raises Family and Morale. Slowly lowers Heat.

4. BANK
Move liquid cash into savings. Safer from raids.

5. STORE
Buy protection (Revolver, Body Armor, etc.). Reduces raid damage and can lower Heat.

6. LAY LOW
Do the bare minimum. Small Heat reduction. Good when Heat is dangerously high.

HUSTLE DETAILS (CHOOSE CAREFULLY)

LEGAL (Zero Heat gain - recommended for long runs):

1. Card Pulls - Buy sealed Pokemon/Sports cards and flip them. Pure gambling. No Heat.

2. Uber / DoorDash - Safe, boring, reliable small income. Lowest risk option.

ILLEGAL / RISKY (Adds Heat + arrest risk):

3. Sell Drugs - Fastest cash. Very high Heat and constant risk of arrest or raids.

4. Boost & Pawn - Steal and flip goods. Good money but Heat rises fast.

WARNING: Illegal hustles feel good short-term but almost always lead to divorce, prison, or foreclosure. Safe + legal play wins dramatically more often.
EVENTS & CONSEQUENCES

Every week random events can happen: family incidents, police raids (especially at high Heat), crypto crashes/pumps, debt problems, or jail time.

High Heat is the #1 killer of runs. Once Heat is over 70-80, bad events start chaining.

LAST STAND

When you hit $0 and still have debt, the game gives you one final desperate choice: Last Stand. Success is rare.

WINNING STRATEGY

From hundreds of thousands of simulated runs, the winning pattern is clear:

  • Do Family Time almost every single week.
  • Stick to legal hustles (Uber + Card Pulls).
  • Only trade when you have genuine surplus cash - and respect the 24h weekly window.
  • Buy protection from the Store when you can.
  • Never let Heat stay high for long.

Safe, boring, consistent play beats risky plays almost every time.

ENDINGS

Keep Family high and Heat low for many weeks -> good family endings.
Let Heat spiral or Family collapse -> divorce, foreclosure, prison, or worse.
