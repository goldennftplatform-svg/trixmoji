// 02 · THE APPLE RULE DOLLARS TIMES DAYS

SOURCE · /learn · THREE WORKED WALLETS

Buy TBB. Hold it. A sell empties the basket.

The page's three steps: every $1 of TBB you hold is one apple. Hold that apple 24 hours and you get one more, free. Drop any apple, meaning sell, and the basket empties. Keep the full basket to stay in the draw. Still holding at the next draw, you are in that draw too. Sell any TBB this season and the wallet leaves every draw. No tickets. No hat. A swap that routes through several pools and ends as a buy counts as a buy.

THE SENTENCE, AND THE ARITHMETIC THAT CHECKS IT

  • Published rule: $1 bought = 1 ticket. Every ticket held 24 hours earns +1 more ticket. A sell drops every ticket you had, and every ticket you would have earned, to 0 for the rest of the season.
  • The worked cycle is 14 days, on the Mythic hat, which is the smallest prize. Early and large: buy $500 on day 1, hold all 14 days, finish with 7,000 tickets. That is 500 times 14.
  • Late and small: buy $100 on day 10, hold through day 14. Day 10 counts, so that is 5 days. 100 times 5 is 500 tickets.
  • Sold out: buy $500 on day 1, sell on day 8, finish at 0. The page says that wallet bought the most of the three and still finishes at zero.
  • 7,000 / 500 = 14. The page's "14x" is that ratio. Buying sooner and holding longer is the whole edge inside one hat.
  • A doubling reading of "each ticket earns one more" would turn $500 into 500 × 2^13 by day 14. The sample wallet is 7,000, so the examples are dollars still held, times days held, with the buy day included.
  • Live tickets are fractional. The Common leader on this read had 148.72 tickets. The rules page does not publish the rounding.

THE EIGHT RULES ON THE PAGE

  • Buy TBB with SOL or USDC, from any app. A swap into the TBB markets counts: this site, Jupiter, Raydium, PumpSwap, Phantom, any wallet or bot. The button on the board routes through Jupiter.
  • Biggest net buyer wins. Each buy is valued in USD at the minute it lands. USDC at $1. SOL at its market price that minute. Score is what you bought minus what you sold during the season.
  • Hold what you buy. If a wallet no longer holds the TBB it net-bought, the score shrinks in proportion. Buying, moving tokens to another wallet, and dumping does not count. TBB staked at stakethebull.xyz counts as held for this board.
  • The apple page does not say a stake is exempt from the apple sell rule. The two contests publish two different hold tests.
  • One wallet, one entry. The wallet that receives the TBB is credited, and its buys stack. Team, creator, and liquidity-pool wallets cannot rank.
  • Final-hour overtime. If rank 1 changes hands during the last hour, one hour is added. That repeats until a leader holds rank 1 through a full final hour. Overtime hours on the feed: 0.
  • Wash trading, self-dealing, and manipulation are excluded. Suspicious activity is held for manual review before payout.
  • The winner has to be reachable through the winning wallet. Claim instructions go up on X @GoingParabolic when the season ends.
