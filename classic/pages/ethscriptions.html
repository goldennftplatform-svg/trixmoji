Launching a collection using escrow

Inscribe each piece, lock it to escrow, and set one price. Primary sales pay the creator 99.5%. Standard resale prices include a 2.5% creator royalty; the seller receives 97.5% and DOSWAPZ receives 0.

  1. Log in on Account and add your Ethereum wallet.
  2. Create each ethscription in that wallet. This page does not do it. The id is the creation transaction.
  3. Fill in Launch and click Launch it. Pick 2.5% or 5% for later resales. That choice stays.
  4. Send each piece to the escrow address with 0 ETH. The hex data is only the ethscription id.
  5. Paste that lock under Add a piece and click Deposit it.
  6. A buyer clicks Mint and pays the mint price. The mint is random. The cover stays blank. You receive 99.5%. When escrow sends the piece to the buyer’s wallet, the picture is revealed. This page does not hold that key.

FEES

Anyone can list or buy. On a resale the price includes 2.5% for the creator. The buyer pays that price. The seller receives 97.5%. DOSWAPZ takes 0. If the creator chose 5%, the buyer pays the ask plus 5.5%, the seller receives the ask, and DOSWAPZ takes 0.5%. A refund returns everything the buyer paid.
