HOW TO RECORD (multi-track)
  1. Use the full 16-step board to program a beat — or hit ★ SAMPLE for 1 of 10 random styles. ▶ PLAY to audition.
  2. On the beat maker, open the SEND → dropdown, pick TR 1–TR 8, then hit ⬇ SEND (puts a full 4-bar clip on that track).
  3. On the track board, click ○ on another track (row turns red = armed for record).
  4. Hit ● RECORD — allow the mic when the browser asks. Other tracks play while you record (overdub).
  5. Hit ● again (or STOP) to finish. Clip appears on that track with a waveform.
  6. Repeat: arm track 3, record, etc. up to 8 tracks. Each track is a separate layer.
  7. Hit ▶ PLAY ALL to hear the full mix. M = mute, S = solo, ⬇ = download, ✕ = clear one track.
  8. MIDI LIBRARY: 30 sounds (keys, bass, leads, pads, drums). Pick a patch, play Z–M / Q–I or a MIDI keyboard, ● REC TAKE, then ⬇ SEND onto a track.
TIP: Only one track can be armed at a time. Recording replaces that track’s clip. Use headphones to avoid mic feedback. First click the page if audio is blocked.
