HOW TO RECORD (each track)
  1. Beat maker: program pads or ★ SAMPLE → pick TR1–TR8 in the SEND dropdown → ⬇ SEND.
  2. Arm: click ○ on another track (row turns red). Only one track armed at a time.
  3. Record: hit ● RECORD, allow mic, perform. Other tracks play under you.
  4. Stop: hit ● again. Waveform shows on that track.
  5. Arm TR3, TR4… up to TR8 for more layers. ▶ plays the full mix. M mute · S solo · ⬇ download · ✕ clear.
TIP: Use headphones. Re-recording a track replaces its clip. CLR clears all 8 tracks.
