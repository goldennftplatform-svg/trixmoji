CHOPPER DADDYINSTRUCTIONS

You run a 16-bit chopper shop. Buy parts, bolt them on, take a customer job, and get paid. Then take the bike out on the desert highway.

BACK TO THE GARAGE PLAY ON DOSWAPZ

CONTROLS

  • W or Up — accelerate on a test ride.
  • S or Down — slow down.
  • A or Left, D or Right — steer.
  • Space — brake.
  • R — leave the highway and return to the garage.
  • The bottom bar has RIDE and GARAGE. HOW TO opens these instructions inside the shop. SOUND turns the shop audio on or off.

CASH AND PARTS

  • A new game starts with $4,850 and a starter bike.
  • The catalog has Frames, Engines, Front End, Wheels, Tanks, Exhaust, and Misc.
  • Click a part to put it on the bike. You pay the difference from the part already fitted. A cheaper part pays the difference back. A short shop refuses the sale.
  • The four garage bikes (Basic Bobber, Custom Chopper, Rat Rod, Long Fork) charge the same way.
  • Fitting a part plays a wrench. Frames, engines, and wheels use a slower ratchet. The paint chip on Build Complete changes the bike color.

EXHAUST

Each pipe has its own sound. Click a pipe in the catalog, or play it here. On a test ride the engine keeps that voice and rises with RPM.

DRAG PIPES — deep open lope.
Low, raspy, long.
UPSWEPT PIPES — bright crack.
Higher, snappy, less bass.
SHOTGUN PIPES — loud wide bark.
The noisiest pipe in the shop.
WRAPPED PIPES — muffled rumble.
Quiet, dull, less rasp.

ASSEMBLY

  • Ten steps: Frame, Engine, Transmission, Primary Drive, Rear Wheel, Front End, Tank, Seat, Exhaust, Controls.
  • Pick a step, then INSTALL STEP. The wrench turns.
  • Submit a customer order only after the frame and engine steps are installed.
  • INSPECT reads the checklist. A long fork asks for a handling review. These are game checks, not a real shop manual.

JOBS

  • Pick a job on the map and press ACCEPT JOB. The customer order updates.
  • ACCEPT / SUBMIT pays that job into your cash and adds reputation.
  • Pays: Local Run $1,200, Hell’s Angel $2,800, Desert Tour $3,500, Show Special $5,200, Outlaw Build $7,500.
  • NEXT CUSTOMER moves to the next order.
  • The goal counter fills at five submitted jobs.

SOUND

  • Sound starts on. The browser stays quiet until the first click or key.
  • You should hear a ratchet when a part or assembly step goes on, a different bark for each exhaust, a cash chime when a job pays, a buzz when a sale is refused, and the bike on the highway, including a shift click and a brake hiss.
  • SETTINGS on the title, and SOUND on the bottom bar, toggle it. The choice is remembered in this browser.

This is a game. It does not replace a real mechanic, a torque spec, or a road-legal inspection.
