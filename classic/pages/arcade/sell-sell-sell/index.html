SELL! SELL! SELL!

Classic TAPPER rules. You are a little Michael Seller on 4 rows.

HOW TO PLAY
• Customers walk in on each bar with a number (how many sells they want).
• Move Michael UP / DOWN between the 4 rows.
• HOLD / TAP POUR to send a $SELLER bag sliding down that row.
• Match their request — fill the number before they reach you.
• Empty bags slide back — catch them or lose a life.
• Hit the quota before time runs out. Miss customers = lose lives.
