01 CHECK IN

PAIR WALLETS. SET PAYOUT.

Official FAQ: Check In confirms rewards go to the right chain. To mine, you need Check In. Connect Solana, verify Solana, connect Ethereum, verify Ethereum, optional X, choose payout wallet. A payout test may send a small amount to prove the destination. Incomplete Check In can change claim priority.

02 MINE

BROWSER PROOF OF WORK. CLAIM wPOND.

Official: once the check-in window completes, mining begins. You need SOL. Activate a rig, hash, then claim. Rewards are not auto-sent until you claim. Difficulty auto-adjusts. Halvings cut rewards 50%. Boost raises hashrate. Official pitch: mine from a laptop or phone, no ASIC.

03 SWAP

DEX VOLUME FEEDS THE REWARD POOL.

Pond swap is a curated DEX. A small fee goes into the reward pool. Referral / Friends links share swap and mine rewards. Not every Jupiter route counts. THIS PAGE's old rails = Jupiter Metis v1 + ~1% into Pond partner fee vaults — that is what Pond's dossier indexes. Plain / ultra Jupiter usually does not.

04 WATER

LOCK wPOND. STREAM pondSOL.

Official Water FAQ: lock longer, bigger pondSOL multiplier. Unlock anytime. Early unlock returns wPOND by lock progress; penalty wPOND goes to miners. Each lock boosts mining; mining health boosts locks. Multiple independent locks allowed. Official site also says mining activity gates how much you can lock.

05 SPAWN

TURN MINED ACTIVITY INTO OTHER TOKENS.

Official: spawn is not a swap. You do not pick the token — you get the registry token with the highest supply at spawn time. Bid/auction page: mined tokens are distributed as Spawn rewards. Community notes (unofficial): ~500M wPOND minimum; events tied to claim counts.

06 AUCTION

BID PNDC. PICK WHAT GETS MINED.

Official bid FAQ: anyone can bid PNDC on an SPL or EVM token with liquidity. Auction starts at 1T PNDC and decays. First bid at the current price wins. 50% of mining fees (hash boosts, rig activations, swaps) go to the winning bidder in SOL. Other 50% liquidity-mines the winning token. Examples listed: PNDC, PORK, PEPE, WIF, wPOND, FartCoin.

LOOP OFFICIAL FAQ + TERMS

THE MACHINE IN ONE LINE

Check In pairs ETH + SOL (and optional X) so claims have a destination. Mining is in-browser proof-of-work that accrues wPOND until you claim. Counted swaps and boosts feed mining health. Claims can open Spawn, where mined activity becomes other tokens. Water locks wPOND for a pondSOL stream and extra mining boost. Auction lets PNDC bids steer which token the cycle liquidity-mines. MINE pond0x.com/mining

ACTIVATE. HASH. CLAIM. HALVING AND DIFFICULTY ARE LIVE KNOBS.

Official copy: “Proof Of Work.” “Activate to start mining.” You need SOL. Difficulty auto-adjusts with demand. Boost increases hashrate. Halving is a periodic 50% cut. Rewards go out when you activate a claim, not as a silent drip. Drifted / pending txs are a real failure mode the official FAQ does not fully specify; community notes say drifted work can wait until a later settlement window.

THIS PAGE's mining box is a live dossier read of health, sessions, mempool, drift, priority. It does not start a Pond mine for you.

WATER pond0x.com/water

LOCK MULTIPLIER. EARLY UNLOCK FEEDS MINERS.

Official: longer lock → greater pondSOL multiplier. Unlock anytime. Progress-based return of wPOND. Penalty share goes to miners. Mining boost per lock, gone on unlock. Mining health boosts the lock. Independent locks stack. Community notes (unofficial): UI “weeks” look like epochs in code; example multipliers 1.0x (1) through 3.7x (49); minimums around 500M wPOND have been observed on the lock UI.

CLUE: water and mine are coupled on purpose. Slash on early exit is part of the loop, not a bug in the FAQ.

BID pond0x.com/mining/bid

50 / 50 ON THE WINNING TOKEN.

Winning bid sets the mined token. Fees from hash boosts, rig activations, and swaps are split: half to the winning bidder in SOL, half buys (liquidity-mines) that token. Spawn then distributes those mined tokens. Minimum bid starts at 1T PNDC and decreases. First at the printed price wins.

CLUE: auction is how the room points the miner. Spawn is how the room gets paid in that token.
