HOW TO RECORD
  1. Program the full 16-step board (or ★ SAMPLE), pick TR1–TR8 in SEND, hit ⬇ SEND.
  2. On the track board, click ○ to arm another track (row turns red).
  3. Hit ● RECORD, allow mic, perform over the beat.
  4. Hit ● again to stop. Arm track 3, 4… up to 8 layers.
  5. ▶ PLAY ALL for the mix. M mute · S solo · ⬇ download · ✕ clear.
  6. MIDI LIBRARY: 30 sounds. Pick a patch, play Z–M / Q–I or a MIDI keyboard, ● REC TAKE, then ⬇ SEND onto a track.
