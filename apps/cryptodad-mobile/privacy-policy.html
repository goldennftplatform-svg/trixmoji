CRYPTO DAD — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: June 17, 2026

Crypto Dad Mobile is a single-player life simulation game published by DOSWAPZ. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

We do not collect, sell, or transmit personal data. Game progress runs in your browser or app shell on your device. No account, email, or login is required to play.

Local storage

The app may store minimal local state (for example, service-worker cache for offline loading) on your device only. This stays on your device and is not sent to DOSWAPZ servers.

Third parties

The mobile app does not embed advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or social login providers. If you tap an external link (for example, to the DOSWAPZ website), that site has its own policies.

Children

Crypto Dad includes mature themes (crime, stress, simulated consequences). It is not directed at children under 13. Parental discretion is advised.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact via official DOSWAPZ channels at doswapz.com.

Changes

We may update this page. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
