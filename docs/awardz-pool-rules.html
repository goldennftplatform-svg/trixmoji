0. STATUS — LIVE ENTRIES

Awardz Pool accepts optional live entries when the on-page lock is open, you have connected with X, acknowledged the PVP / own-risk boxes, created an intent, and sent native SOL or ETH to the published address for the exact amount shown. Arcade play remains free without entering.

There is no line-item fee. Your stake is booked in USD at the live oracle at the moment of the intent. At lock close the USD book is paid in crypto at the then-spot. If crypto went up, leftover coins stay with DOSWAPZ (conversion difference). If crypto went down, the lock pays 100% of holdings — nobody is owed a dollar top-up.

Unsolicited transfers that are not matched to a live Awardz intent (wrong amount, no intent, mint/launch payments) are not contest entries and need not be returned.

1. VOLUNTARY — PLAY STAYS FREE

  • Every arcade cabinet can be played without Awardz, without a wallet, and without a seed.
  • Entering Awardz is an extra opt-in. No dark pattern. No “connect to play.”
  • You may close the page and keep playing for entertainment only. Scores without an Awardz entry have no cash value (Legal §11).

2. THIS IS PVP

Awardz Pool is player-versus-player. Entrants compete against each other on high score at the end of a day, week, or month lock. The prize pool is other players’ voluntary entries (plus your own entry if you win). DOSWAPZ is the contest operator / administrator, not your opponent, not a bookmaker taking the other side of your bet, and not a house bank betting against you.

  • Winner (or documented tie split) is the eligible player with the highest score in that lock, subject to these rules and Legal §11A.
  • Skill, practice, and the game client matter. Chance, latency, bugs, and cheats can still affect outcomes. There is no warranty of a fair or skill-pure match.
  • DOSWAPZ does not promise you will win, place, or recover any value.

5. ENTRY, LOCKS, USD BOOK

  • Locks: day (closes 00:00 UTC), week (Monday 00:00 UTC), month (1st 00:00 UTC). A send that confirms after close does not enter that lock.
  • You may enter more than one lock only with separate confirmed intents and sends.
  • Minimum advertised USD worth is a display floor (preview: $5). The unit of pooling is USD so SOL and ETH can sit in one book. You still send native SOL or ETH to the published DOSWAPZ address shown on the live page.
  • USD figures are estimates from an oracle at deposit and again at settlement. They are not a promise to pay dollars, stablecoins, or a fixed coin amount.

6. SCORING, FREEZE, WINNER

  • Eligible = live matched intent + confirmed on-chain entry into that lock + at least one score posted during the lock window under your X session (or other identity the live page requires).
  • At close the period board freezes. Highest eligible score wins. Ties split the period inventory pro-rata as documented on the live page.
  • Scores are client-posted today and can be spoofed, botted, or wrong. See §9.

7. PAYOUT; CONVERSION LEFTOVER

  • If armed, payout goes to the same address that entered. No redirect. You must be able to receive the asset(s) the period still holds.
  • The period pays from crypto actually held for that lock. If the USD book is larger than the inventory’s then-value, the period pays 100% of holdings and the house leftover is zero. Nobody is owed extra coins from operating funds.
  • If inventory covers the USD book at the settlement oracle, leftover coins may remain with DOSWAPZ as conversion difference / administrative consideration for running the contest — not a line-item “fee,” not an investment return to you, and not a guaranteed house profit.
  • Auto-pay may stay off. Operator may pay manually. Failed sends can be retried or marked needs-manual. Delayed payout is not a breach of a dollar obligation, because there is no dollar obligation.

8. WALLET, COMSEC, OPSEC, AUTO-DISCONNECT

  • COMSEC. HTTPS only. DOSWAPZ never asks for a seed phrase, mnemonic, or private key. Any seed prompt is a scam. Do not paste secrets into chat or forms.
  • OPSEC. Use a dedicated burner wallet funded with only the intended entry. Never your main bag. Bookmark the real host. Check the URL. Watch clipboard attacks and fake extensions.
  • AUTO-DISCONNECT. Same as math mint / phunk / pond swap: connect → sign/send → wallet disconnects. No silent reconnect. Next entry needs a fresh user click.
  • NATIVE SEND ONLY. SOL or ETH value. No token approve. No unlimited allowance for Awardz.
  • Verify recipient, chain, amount, lock, and USD estimate on screen, then again on a block explorer, before you confirm. After broadcast it is yours to live with.
