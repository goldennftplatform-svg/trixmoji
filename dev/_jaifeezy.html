About the Artist

Human
First.
Always.

jAI Feezy is me turning ideas into sound without waiting for permission or a studio setup. It started as me experimenting with AI just to see what it could do, but it turned into a way of making music that feels closer to how I actually think. I don't stay in one genre because I don't listen in one genre, so the music moves the same way.

This isn't about replacing real artists or pretending machines make art on their own. It's just me using every tool I can get my hands on to build something that hits a feeling first. Some tracks come out wild, some come out clean, but the goal is always the same — make something that sticks with you after it stops playing.
